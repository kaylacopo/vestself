2. What Vest Self does

Vest Self helps you achieve your goals. You tell us what you want to achieve, and we:

  • use AI to break your goal into a plan of daily tasks;
  • add those tasks to your calendar, if you connect it;
  • help you track your progress and verify what you've completed;
  • let you invite an accountability partner and share your progress with the Vest Self community;
  • let you put money behind your goal, which you earn back as you follow through.

We're constantly improving Vest Self, so features may change over time.

5. Commitments

A commitment is money you choose to put behind a goal, to give yourself a real reason to follow through. Commitments are completely optional — you can use Vest Self without one.

Before you commit

Before you confirm, the app shows you the amount, the tasks and milestones it's tied to, your deadline, any grace days you have, any fees, and the most you could lose. Nothing is charged until you confirm.

Paying

When you confirm a commitment, the amount is charged to your card. Payments are processed by Stripe, and we never see or store your full card number.

Earning it back

As you complete your tasks and milestones on time, you earn your commitment back in stages. You can earn back up to 100% of your commitment. If you complete everything on time, you get all of it back.

A commitment is not an investment, a deposit, savings, or a bet. It earns no interest or return, and you can never get back more than you put in.

6. Missed tasks and penalties

Because there are penalties for not completing tasks on time, we can't guarantee you'll earn back the full 100%.

What counts as a missed task

A task is missed if it isn't completed and verified by its deadline, and isn't made up within any grace days or redemption window shown in the app.

Every missed task is reviewed

Money only moves after a missed task has been reviewed. When a task is marked as missed, we'll let you know and give you the chance to submit proof or tell us what happened. No penalty is applied until that review is complete.

If a penalty applies

If the review confirms the task was missed, you lose the part of your commitment tied to that task, and that amount can no longer be earned back. The app always shows you how much you've earned back and how much is still at stake.

Money lost to penalties is kept by Vest Self and is not refunded.

7. Refunds

  • If a refund to your card fails — for example, because the card has expired — we'll contact you to sort it out.
  • If we stop offering Vest Self, we'll refund the full unearned balance of any active commitment.
