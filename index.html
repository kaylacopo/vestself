    Accountability, your way

    Whatever makes you show up, we've got your back.

    Not everyone needs the same kind of motivation. That's why Vest Self gives you different ways to stay accountable.

    Accountability partner

    A friend who holds you to it.

    Bring someone into your corner who knows what you're working towards and helps you stay true to your word.

    Community

    People who get it.

    Surround yourself with people chasing their own goals, sharing progress, and celebrating each other's wins.

    Streaks and check-ins

    A reason to keep checking in.

    Turn intentions into scheduled actions, build a streak, and make every completed commitment count.

    Optional stakes

    Put something on the line.

    Want an extra push? Choose a financial commitment that makes skipping a little harder.

    Your ambition is required. Your money isn't.

    Vest Self works without putting any money down. If you want an extra push, you can choose to back your goal. You'll see exactly how it works before you commit, and nothing is charged until you confirm.

    Invest mode
    Put in an amount you choose and earn it back as you show up. The most you can lose is 20%.
    Hard mode
    Choose a penalty for each missed session. Miss enough sessions and you could lose the full amount.

    Optional, always. Read the terms

    Our promise

    We don't profit from your forfeits. We want to see you win.

    Sometimes, having something to lose can help you follow through. Not because you deserve to be punished for missing a day, but because meaningful commitments can change what you do when motivation fades.

    Putting money on the line is always optional. Our aim is simple: more people achieving their goals, with fewer forfeits.

    When forfeits happen, our commitment is to put that value back into the community to help motivate someone else's progress.

    Because progress is the point. Not punishment.
